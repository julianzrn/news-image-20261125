## Chosen stories (each verified against ≥2 major outlets)

1. **LEAD — U.S. tariffs.** The Trump administration proposed new
   tariffs on 14 major trading partners after an eight-month Section
   301 inquiry into structural excess industrial capacity; China faces
   the highest rates, a proposed 20% excess-capacity duty on top of
   roughly 25% from the first term; a hearing is scheduled and
   implementation could slip into January; Beijing called overcapacity
   a "false issue".
   - Reuters, 25 Nov
     (https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-proposes-new-tariffs-14-trading-partners-fight-excess-factory-capacity-2026-11-25/);
     Al Jazeera, 25 Nov
     (https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/trump-announces-new-trade-tariffs-which-countries-will-they-target).
2. **RAS TANURA — Houthi claim.** Yemen's Houthis said they attacked
   the Ras Tanura refinery, the kingdom's largest, and that the strike
   caused fires. The Saudi-led coalition said air defences destroyed
   four drones over Ras Tanura and did not confirm a hit. NASA/Planet
   satellite imagery showed thick black smoke over the site; oil prices
   rose more than 2%.
   - Reuters, 25 Nov (via Hydrocarbon Processing, 25 Nov, and the
     Reuters post quoted by the Times of Israel, 25 Nov);
     Gulf News live coverage, 25 Nov
     (https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-conflict-trump-says-iran-war-funded-through-january-amid-saudi-yemen-escalation-1.500759977).
   - Headline attributes the strike to the Houthis ("Houthis Say…");
     the fires are the Houthis' claim, not stated as confirmed fact.
3. **JIUQUAN — Shenzhou-24.** Muhammad Zeeshan Ali became the first
   foreign astronaut to board China's Tiangong space station, arriving
   aboard Shenzhou-24 with Tang Hongbo and Wu Yi after launching from
   Jiuquan at 08:18 Beijing time and docking about three and a half
   hours later. The 40-year-old Pakistan Air Force payload specialist
   will spend about a week in orbit on joint science experiments.
   - Reuters, 25 Nov (photos and reporting carried by Geo.tv and CNA);
     Dawn, 25 Nov (https://www.dawn.com/news/print/2046753);
     Xinhua via SCIO, 25 Nov
     (http://english.scio.gov.cn/chinavoices/2026-11/25/content_118789123.html).
4. **CANBERRA — Solomon Islands treaty.** Solomon Islands signed a
   treaty in Canberra making Australia its primary security partner,
   pledging no foreign military bases on its soil and consultation with
   Canberra on sensitive infrastructure; Australia will provide A$740
   million over ten years, including support for free schooling.
   - Al Jazeera, 25 Nov
     (https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/solomon-islands-signs-security-deal-with-australia);
     CNN, 25 Nov
     (https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/24/australia/solomon-islands-australia-security-pact-intl-hnk);
     Nikkei Asia, 25 Nov
     (https://asia.nikkei.com/politics/international-relations/solomon-islands-chooses-australia-as-primary-security-partner);
     Australian PM's office, 25 Nov
     (https://www.pm.gov.au/media/signing-australia-solomon-islands-treaty).
5. **UKRAINE — brief (text only).** Russian strikes killed at least
   five people and cut power in five regions (Dnipropetrovsk, Donetsk,
   Zaporizhia, Odesa, Kharkiv); the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant lost
   external power and switched to diesel generators. A Ukrainian drone
   attack in Russia's Samara region killed one person and cut power.
   - Al Jazeera, 25 Nov
     (https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/11/25/russian-strikes-on-ukraine-cause-power-outages-in-five-regions);
     Reuters World News Summary, 25 Nov (via Devdiscourse).
   - No photograph run: the refinery names (Kuibyshev/Syzran) were
     reported by monitoring channels and Reuters explicitly could not
     independently verify them, so they are not named in the copy.
